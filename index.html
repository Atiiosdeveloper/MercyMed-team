About MercyMed

MercyMed is a private outpatient medical centre in Bishkek. Local and international doctors work together to give high-quality care based on modern medical knowledge and international experience.

Our story

For over 10 years, Dr. Jonathan Williams (now our chief doctor) and Mahabat Alieva (a Kyrgyz nurse, now our director) have run mobile clinics in villages across Kyrgyzstan. In October 2023 we opened our clinic in Bishkek to continue and expand this work: a place where patients can come back for follow-up and tests, a permanent team for the mobile clinics, and our own medical licence.

What we bring to Kyrgyzstan

  • Evidence-based medicine: decisions based on research and international experience
  • Care for the whole family in one place, with no need to visit many specialists
  • No unnecessary tests or medicines – you only pay for what helps
  • Training for local doctors and medical students

Mobile clinics & charitable work

Mobile clinics are the heart of MercyMed. For over 10 years our team has travelled to villages in all regions of Kyrgyzstan, for a single day or for a whole week.

  • A team of 4–5 doctors, a nurse, a receptionist and a pharmacy helper
  • Consultations, medicines and health advice for people with little access to doctors
  • We come when a village, health centre or local community invites us

Frequently asked questions

        

          Do I need to see a doctor before an ultrasound or lab test?
          
Yes. We like you to see a doctor first, so you only get the tests you really need.

        

        

          Do you keep patients overnight?
          
No. MercyMed is an outpatient clinic. We can give medicines and IV infusions, but patients go home the same day.

        

        

          Which languages do your doctors speak?
          
Kyrgyz, Russian and English. Some of our doctors also consult in Chinese, German or French.

        

        

          Is there a discount on lab tests?
          
Yes. MercyMed patients get 30% off all tests at Express Plus, the laboratory inside our clinic.

        

        

          How do I get my lab results?
          
Express Plus gives you a QR code to open your results. You can then send them to us on WhatsApp.

        

        

          How do I book an appointment?
          
Use the booking form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. Our receptionist replies and confirms a time.
